## Lexical: the French word must be French, not English in disguise
1. rev 1689 (revealing, cue 460). EN: "it must be authoritative." Bad: "elle doit être autoritaire." Fixed: "elle doit faire
   autorité." Why: autoritaire = domineering.
2. rev 1683 (revealing, cue 101). EN: "he receives submissions, manuscripts". Bad: "des soumissions". Fixed: "des
   propositions". Why: calque; a manuscript sent to a publisher is not a "soumission".
3. rev 1607 (christmas, cue 643). EN: "We worship You." Bad: "Nous te vénérons." Fixed: "Nous te rendons un culte." Why:
   vénérer is for saints, not God.
4. rev 1514 (peace, cue 614). EN: "Faith is primarily thinking, thinking." Bad: "La foi est avant tout une pensée, une
   pensée." Fixed: "La foi, c'est avant tout penser, penser." Why: the activity, not "a thought".
5. rev 1506 (peace, cue 428). EN: "In the face of sin-bearing". Bad: "Face au port du péché". Fixed: "Face au fait de porter
   le péché". Why: noun calque.
6. rev 1500 (peace, cue 234). EN: "that frees God to forgive you". Bad: "cela libère Dieu pour vous pardonner". Fixed: "cela
   permet à Dieu de vous pardonner". Why: calque of "frees X to".
7. rev 1390 (word, cue 197). EN: "He is the qualifier for all prayer". Bad: "celui qui qualifie toute prière". Fixed: "celui
   qui rend toute prière recevable". Why: qualifier here = makes acceptable, not describes.
8. rev 1388 (word, cue 67). EN: "We will touch His wound prints." Bad: "ses marques de blessures". Fixed: "les marques de
   ses plaies". Why: word-for-word compound.
9. rev 1376 (passover, cue 299). EN: "what He pledged to them". Bad: "ce qu'il leur a engagé". Fixed: "ce qu'il leur a
   garanti". Why: "engager" does not take that construction.
10. rev 1568 (inability, cue 564). EN: "the Council at Dort". Bad: "le concile de Dordrecht". Fixed: "le synode de
    Dordrecht". Why: Reformed assembly = synode.
11. rev 1393 (word, cue 588). EN: "replace this word comprehend". Bad: "ce mot comprendre". Fixed: "ce mot anglais
    « comprehend »". Why: he discusses the English word; Segond's verse says "reçue".
12. sproul-100 a-contrite cue 68. EN: "the demand of His righteousness, His justice be satisfied". Bad: "l'exigence de
    sa justice, de sa justice". Fixed: "l'exigence de sa droiture, de sa justice". Why: two DISTINCT English words must
    stay distinct — never collapse them into one French word repeated back-to-back (the gates' adjacent_duplicate warn).
13. sproul-100 a-consuming-fire cue 154. EN: "the glory of God shone round about them". Bad: "la gloire du Seigneur".
    Fixed: "la gloire de Dieu". Why: keep the speaker's own word — God is not interchangeable with Lord/Seigneur.

## Mistranslation: read the meaning, not the words

    argument.
23. rev 1557 (inability, cue 283). EN: "the way humans came from God." Bad: "la façon dont les humains sont sortis de Dieu."
    Fixed: "la condition dans laquelle les humains sont sortis des mains de Dieu." Why: "sortis de Dieu" = emanation.
24. rev 1511 (peace, cue 545). EN: "peace in your life is pursued through righteousness". Bad: "la paix ... se poursuit par

25. rev 1576 (hell, cue 49). EN: "a homosexual advocate." Bad: "une militante homosexuelle." Fixed: "une militante de la
    cause homosexuelle." Why: she advocates a cause; the bad version says what she is.
26. rev 1379 + 1380 (passover, cues 451-452). EN: "our sovereign God overrules ... their choices." Bad: "surpasse". Fixed:
    "l'emporte ... sur leurs choix". Why: overrule = prevail over, not surpass.

    flips the joke into the opposite meaning.
